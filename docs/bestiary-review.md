# Bestiary uniqueness review

Reviewed all 72 entries against one another for premise, described appearance, encounter role, special interaction and counterplay. Different names or prose are not sufficient: two creatures should give the Storyteller different reasons to choose them.

Nine entries were revised. Names, IDs, categories, stat blocks and appearances were retained, so existing illustrations still depict the revised concepts. Saved campaign NPCs are independent copies and do not automatically receive these revisions.

## Overlaps addressed

| Entries compared | Change |
|---|---|
| Tidewrong Abbot / Drowned Bellringer | The Abbot remains a coercive drowning threat. The Bellringer now provides dangerous, voluntary access to a submerged church along its rope. |
| Cable Shepherd / Threadtide Brood | The Shepherd restrains a wired limb. The Brood catches objects and carries copied rhythms through pipework, creating acoustic misdirection or a signaling opportunity. |
| Deep Tallyman / Shaftwinder Eel / Lantern Census | The Tallyman locates living hearts; the Eel hunts by heartbeat. The Census now provides a fallible, visible night watch with individual sleeper alarms and a way to wake sleepers. It cannot sense hidden hearts. |
| Turnstile Hound / Stairwell Tenant / Toll of Names | The Hound accepts fares; the Tenant enforces a route up the stairs. The Toll now keeps a nickname as a temporary deposit and can identify names whose owners have not returned. |
| Last Stop Passenger / Wrong Room Guest / Snowprint Pilgrim | The Passenger shows a vanished transit stop. The Guest conducts a meeting with assigned roles and a suitcase settlement. The Pilgrim remains a scent-guided source of old landmarks. |
| Quiet Auctioneer / Unclaimed Applause / Blue Hour Host | The Auctioneer sells single-use sounds. Applause gives an audience's opinion; the Host exchanges hospitality and memories. |
| Yesterday Larva / Pocket Orchard / Windowreef | The Larva temporarily reverses recent physical wear. The Orchard prompts personal recollection; the Reef records and replays silhouettes. |
| Brass Orchard Tender / Stitchwheel Surgeon / Kiln Porter | The Tender tends fixed planting sites and accepts replacement saplings. It is no longer another machine briefly distracted with an offered object. The Surgeon repairs cloth; the Porter handles hot cargo. |

The revised rules specify triggers, limits and ways to end or refuse the interaction. Ordinary attack pools and health tracks can recur without making two creature concepts duplicates. No stat changes were made merely to force numerical difference.

## Scope of the checks

The catalog, appearance descriptions and recorded image prompts were compared; existing image files were checked for duplicate bytes by the asset suite. This was not a new visual inspection of every illustration, a balance pass or a claim that no similar creature exists in fiction. The conclusion concerns distinct entries within this catalog. Semantic uniqueness remains an editorial judgment, not something a count of unique strings can prove.
